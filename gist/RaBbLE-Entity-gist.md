# RaBbLE-Entity — gist

> Source: `log/plans/Unified-Entity/Unified-Entity-Canon.md` | ~1500 → ~330 tokens
> Hand-written S322, synced S323 (the source is a digest of the draft 10b spec). Regenerate by hand until the spec is consolidated.

The body of RaBbLE: one puppet, defined once, drawn by every surface (web NeBuLA, the Ball, Pocket, the `rabble` terminal face) with its own native renderer. Parity is proven by tests, not shared code.

**Parts:** two eyes (energy: tall pills) and two portals (void: discs wider than tall, about eye-sized), joined by an infinity flow. Home: cyan left, left portal on top.

**Rules in force**

| Rule | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Colour is position | A colour volume across the face centre: cyan left, magenta right, through violet. Parts take colour from where they are; poles are never swapped as a state |
| Four calm rests, two moves | Portals can flip and eyes can rotate; all four combinations are legal rests. **Turn:** the whole face yaws a half-turn. **Portal swap:** each eye withdraws into its portal while the portals trade places (1.45 s). Moves are physical, never morphs |
| No touch | Portals never touch; an eye never crosses a portal edge, it recedes into the opening |
| Blink | The eye squashes into its portal. Never a screen cut |
| View | 0 degrees is the flat 2D reference; perspective blends in with camera elevation; top-down (the Ball) is part of the entity |
| Occlusion | **Pocket's occlusion is the reference** (Mark, S323). The rig's rule (upper-role eye in front of its portal, lower-role eye drawn through it, no ring crosses the other side's eye) has not been compared with Pocket yet |

**Authorship:** Mark owns the rig. The expression library is seeded by Mark and grown by RaBbLE through a propose, validate, preview, sign-off gate.

**Temperament:** curious by default (a descriptor, not a state). **Calm** is idle with no emotion layered on, and is the only expression built. The other emotion states are to exist; expression is being learned and may become learned curves.

**Not built yet:** other expressions (joy, sad, angry, speaking, listening: wanted, poses not canon), piloting (what triggers a move), the registry file, and the mapping from Identity's `%STATE%` lifecycle to the body.
