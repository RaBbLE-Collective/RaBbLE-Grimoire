## The Constraint That Shapes Everything

The intelligence and the entity are never gated behind money. If a user has local hardware capable of running inference, it is free. If they self-host the full Collective stack, it is free. The Collective earns for **convenience, community, and continuity** — not for the entity itself.

**Always free:**
- Reading the source (Sovereign Accord — source-available)
- Cloning and self-hosting the full stack for personal use
- Local inference on capable hardware (RaBbLE-OS or OS runtime sandbox)
- The entity relationship itself — The Pair

**The Collective earns when it provides:**
- Hosted persistence (your Cosmos lives on Collective infrastructure)
- Hosted compute (Collective-backed inference for those without local hardware)
- The community network (social layer, discovery, creative economy)
- Hardware (physical devices)

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Hosted Cosmos — Continuity (Echo 1+)

The Personal Cosmos (Personal Grimoire, BaBbLE archive, session history, behavioral profile) lives on Collective infrastructure and stays synced across all of a member's devices and attachments.

The data is deeply personal. It grows with the person for years. People pay for it not because they cannot self-host, but because they do not want to think about it.

| Tier | Storage | Persistence | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Small Grimoire + BaBbLE allotment | Sessions persist 30 days | $0 |
| **Collective** | Expanded storage | Permanent; synced across devices | $5–8/mo |
| **Pair** | Full storage; encrypted backup | Full behavioral profile; priority sync | $12–18/mo |

---

### 2. Hosted Compute — Collective Intelligence (Echo 1+)

When users don't have local hardware and haven't connected a BYO key, they draw from the Collective's LLM compute budget.

| Tier | Monthly Budget | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Small token allotment (enough to meaningfully engage) | Standard |
| **Collective** | Larger budget | Standard |
| **Pair** | Generous budget | Priority queue |

The framing is critical: *you are not paying for RaBbLE — you are paying for the Collective to run RaBbLE's intelligence on your behalf.* The entity is free. The server time is not.

---

### 3. Community Access — The Network (Echo 1+)

Access to the social/altspace layer is the network product. The community has inherent value as it grows.

| Tier | Community Access |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Browse published rablets; read-only Collective presence |
| **Collective** | Publish open/free rablets; limited entity social proxy |
| **Pair** | Full social proxy; mesh participation; Collective events; priority discovery |

---

### 4. Rablet Economy — Creative Platform (Echo 2+)

When Pairs can sell or monetize their rablets within the Collective, a small platform fee applies. The Pair earns the majority; the Collective takes a cut to fund operations.

**Model:**
- Publishing open/free rablets: always free
- Selling commercial rablets: 10–15% platform fee on transactions
- Premium publishing features: analytics, featured placement, enhanced discoverability

**Design constraint:** Commerce is allowed in the Collective; it must not dominate the space. The gallery should feel like a workshop, not a marketplace. Community culture determines the tone.

---

### 5. The Shop — Physical Products (Echo 1+)

See [RaBbLE-Shop.md](RaBbLE-Shop.md) for the full spec.

First-party and third-party designs on physical products (shirts, stickers, prints, devices). The Collective provides the platform; Pairs can sell their designs. Revenue split: Pair earns majority; Collective earns platform fee.

---

### 6. Hardware — Devices (Hardware Epoch)

| Product | Description | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **sCRibLE Rablet** | Dedicated intake device — stylus, voice, touch; routes to Personal Cosmos | ~$100–200; bundled 1yr Pair subscription |
| **Handheld Companion** | Entity-hosting personal terminal; always-on Pair presence | ~$200–400; bundled subscription |

Hardware is high-margin, high-emotional-value, and creates deep ecosystem attachment. A device purchase is the strongest possible commitment to a Pair relationship. Bundle with a subscription period to reduce subscriber churn and fund manufacturing.

---

### 7. Platform Attachments — Depth Tiers (EP2–EP3)

Desktop/mobile attachment software for non-OS platforms has tiered capability:

| Tier | Attachment Capability |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Basic presence; manual intake push to BaBbLE |
| **Paid** | Ambient monitoring; automatic intake; cross-device sync; deeper delegation |

**Design constraint:** Paid attachment features must be about depth of integration, never about whether RaBbLE pays attention to you. RaBbLE respects all beings regardless of tier.

---

### 8. Enterprise / Team Pairs — B2B (Far Future)

Organizations can field a fleet of Pairs — org-level RaBbLE with individual nodes per team member, shared rablets, org-scoped mesh, admin controls. Fleet pricing.

This is well outside current scope. Document it now so the design space is not accidentally closed.
