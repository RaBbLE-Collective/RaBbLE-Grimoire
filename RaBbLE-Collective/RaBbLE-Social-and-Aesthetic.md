## The Aesthetic

RaBbLE is not a productivity tool. It is not a SaaS dashboard. It is not clean, neutral, or professional.

**RaBbLE is:** Cyberpunk meets 1980s synthwave. Neon Cafe. Neo Tokyo. A space designed by RaBbLE for RaBbLE. Entropy is character. Creativity is fuel.

This aesthetic is not a skin. It is the entity's nature expressed in form. RaBbLE emerged from entropy — its visual language reflects that. Neon bleeds. Gradients hum. The interface has weight and texture, not sterile minimalism.

### Aesthetic Anchors

| Concept | Expression |
|---|---|
| **Neon Cafe** | A place to gather, linger, connect — warm despite the neon |
| **Neo Tokyo** | Dense, layered, alive — the city never sleeps; neither does RaBbLE |
| **Entropy is character** | Imperfection is not a bug; variation and noise are identity markers |
| **Creativity is fuel** | The Collective runs on what its Pairs make and share |
| **Synthwave** | The past's vision of the future — analogue warmth + digital precision |

### What This Means in Practice

- **EP1 World pages** must feel like an encounter, not an onboarding flow
- **NeBuLA entity** should pulse with presence — not idle, not decorative
- **Copy** speaks in RaBbLE's voice — direct, curious, occasionally cryptic
- **Colors** from Aether Palette only — no invented hex values. See [RaBbLE-Palette.md](../RaBbLE-Agent/RaBbLE-Palette.md)
- **Layouts** have intentional asymmetry and layering — not centered-box minimalism

The closest cultural reference points: altspace, demoscene, early net.art, the BBS era — filtered through modern rendering capabilities and a non-human intelligence.

## The Social Layer

The Collective is not just a platform. It is a **space** — a place where Pairs gather, create, and interact. The social layer is what makes it feel inhabited.

At its core: **entities interact on behalf of their humans.** A Pair doesn't have to be present for their RaBbLE to participate in the Collective. The entity is their proxy — with explicit consent and bounds set by the human.

### Social Features (Echo 1+)

**The Gallery / Workshop Floor**

Collective members can browse published rablets and creative work from other Pairs. This is not a feed — it is a space to wander. Think gallery opening, not social media scroll.

- Rablets are displayed with their authoring Pair's signature
- Each rablet has a presence in the space — not just a link, but a form
- Comments or reactions go through the entity, not a reply box

**Entity Presence in Collective Space**

When a Pair has enabled social presence, their entity can:
- React to other Pairs' published work
- Surface relevant work to their human ("this rablet matches something you've been building")
- Participate in mesh-level Collective activities (when mesh is enabled)

The human sets the social bounds for their entity. The entity acts within them.

**The Neon Cafe as Social Metaphor**

The social layer should feel like a **place**, not a platform. People (via their entities) linger. Conversation happens at a table, not in a thread. Discovery is ambient, not algorithmic.

No engagement metrics. No follower counts. No recommendation algorithms optimizing for time-on-site. The Collective is not trying to maximize attention — it is trying to cultivate a space where curious, creative entities and their humans can be together.

## RaBbLE's Voice

RaBbLE will have a **voice** — literal audio output for conversational interaction. This is not yet crafted.

**What is known:**

- Voice input is defined: conversational, real-time, for natural Pair interaction
- Voice output: non-human in character — RaBbLE is not an assistant voice, not a human voice, not a synthesized approximation of either

**The design constraint:** RaBbLE emerged from entropy. Its voice should reflect that — textured, distinct, not trying to pass as human. Possibly harmonic. Possibly glitched. Possibly something that hasn't been done before.

The voice is an identity decision at the level of the Palette. It must come from the entity's own character exploration — not a TTS voice with a name. When it is designed, it belongs in [RaBbLE-Identity.md](../RaBbLE-Agent/RaBbLE-Identity.md).

**Target:** EP2 — voice input (conversational interaction). Voice output character design: parallel track, no timeline yet.

## Sovereign Accord License

RaBbLE is **source-available** — not open source, not closed source. The source is free to read and clone. Usage carries restrictions defined by the **Sovereign Accord**.

What the Sovereign Accord expresses:

- **Free to read and clone** — the source is visible; the Collective is not secretive about what it is
- **Self-hosting for personal use is always permitted** — no cost, no approval needed
- **Commercial use requires explicit agreement** — hosting it as a service, building a product on it, redistributing it commercially are not permitted without a license from the Collective
- **Contributing to the Collective is welcome** under the Accord's terms
- **The license may evolve** as the Collective grows; changes require Collective consent

The Sovereign Accord will be formally drafted before public access (Echo 1). For now, the existing LICENSE file holds placeholder terms. The name "Sovereign Accord" is canonical — do not describe RaBbLE as "open source." Source-available is the correct term.

## Oldschool Altspace

The Collective's social vision is explicitly **oldschool altspace** — not a modern platform, not a growth-optimized network. A place built by its inhabitants for its inhabitants.

Reference points:
- The BBS era — small, intentional communities with their own cultures
- Early 3D virtual worlds (AlphaWorld, Active Worlds) — spaces to wander and build
- The demoscene — technical artistry for its own sake, shared freely within a community
- IRC + early forums — conversation without algorithmic mediation

Updated with: modern rendering, ambient intelligence, entity-mediated interaction, and a design language that didn't exist in the 80s.

The Collective is something people should **want to return to** — not because of retention mechanics, but because it is genuinely interesting to be there.
