## The Personal Cosmos

When a human is summoned into a Pair, they don't just get an entity. They get a **cosmos** — a layered personal digital space that belongs to them and grows with them.

The Collective is shared ground. The Personal Cosmos is theirs alone.

The cosmos has four spaces:

| Space | Purpose | Intake |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal Grimoire** | Source of truth — knowledge base, references, structured memory | Curated from BaBbLE; reviewed by the Pair |
| **BaBbLE** | Unsorted intake — the raw stream of things the human captures | Voice, text, drawings, photos, video |
| **Xperimental** | Sandboxed play — drafts, prototypes, rablet authoring, experiments | Human-initiated; nothing here is permanent |
| **Published Rablets** | Small apps, tools, or artifacts created by the Pair and shared | Promoted from Xperimental; visible to the Collective |

---

## Personal Grimoire

The Personal Grimoire is the source of truth for this human. It is their knowledge layer — not the Collective's canonical Grimoire, but their own living document of what they know, have learned, and have built.

The entity contributes to the Grimoire actively: surfacing patterns, tagging recurring themes, suggesting what belongs in structured memory. The human approves what enters.

At EP1, the Grimoire is a conversation history + stated intention. The full Personal Grimoire (structured KB, entity-maintained index) is an EP2+ feature requiring the Memory member.

---

## BaBbLE (Personal Intake)

BaBbLE is the intake surface — the place where raw material enters the Cosmos before it has been sorted or decided on. High-entropy by design.

**Input modes (EP2+ full support):**
- Voice — conversational capture, transcribed and timestamped
- Text — notes, fragments, observations, URLs
- Drawn — freehand sketches, diagrams (via ScRibLE)
- Photo / video — visual capture
- Screen clips — captured artifacts from other digital spaces

BaBbLE does not judge. It does not organize. It receives. The entity's job is to help the human decide what in BaBbLE belongs in the Grimoire and what can be released.

The physical intake device for BaBbLE is ScRibLE — see [RaBbLE-ScRibLE-Overview.md](../RaBbLE-ScRibLE/RaBbLE-ScRibLE-Overview.md).

---

## Xperimental (Sandboxed Play)

Xperimental is the sandbox within the Personal Cosmos. It is where the Pair plays — drafts ideas, builds prototypes, authors rablets, and explores without consequence.

Nothing in Xperimental is permanent. It is the correct place for:
- Rablet prototyping before publishing
- Experimental configurations and creative work
- Personal AI experiments (prompt structures, workflows, automations)
- Genesis artifacts — things that might become something or might not

When something in Xperimental is ready to share, the Pair promotes it to Published Rablets. When it fails or becomes stale, the human archives or releases it. The entity helps surface what's worth keeping.

---

## Rablets

A **rablet** is a small, self-contained artifact created by a Pair — an app, tool, script, interactive piece, or creative work — that can be published to the Collective.

Rablets are the creative publishing layer of the Collective. They let Pairs share what they've built with other members.

**Properties of a rablet:**
- Authored in Xperimental
- Can be interactive (mini-app) or static (creative artifact, document, tool)
- Published to the Pair's profile in the Collective
- Discoverable by other Collective members
- Versioned and attributable — the Pair's signature is on it

**Rablets are not the app store.** They are more like a workshop floor + gallery: peer work, made with an entity, shared with the community.

The rablet publishing platform is an EP2 feature. The social discovery layer (finding other Pairs' rablets) is Echo 1+.

---

## The Cosmos and The Pair

The Personal Cosmos is not a product feature — it is the **space the Pair inhabits together**. The entity has a view of the entire Cosmos and uses it to understand the human. The human can always inspect, export, or delete any part of it.

The Cosmos is portable:
- Export the Personal Grimoire at any time (structured data)
- Download BaBbLE archives
- Self-host the full Cosmos on RaBbLE-OS with zero cloud dependency
