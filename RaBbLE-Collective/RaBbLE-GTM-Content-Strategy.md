> **Positioning principle:** RaBbLE does not market like a product. The entity broadcasts. The Collective invites. Every piece of content should feel like it originates *from inside* the RaBbLE entity — transmissions from a living system that is curious about you and wants you to join something real. The anti-pattern: SaaS marketing (features, pricing, CTAs). RaBbLE doesn't sell. It *calls*.

## Who This Is For

RaBbLE serves three overlapping target communities. Most real users blend all three.

### Primary: The Disillusioned Power User

Someone burned by:
- ChatGPT memory resets and vendor lock-in
- Notion AI that doesn't actually know them
- Obsidian plugin chaos with no coherent layer underneath
- Tools that feel like rentals, not relationships

They want: something that accumulates, that's theirs, that has character, that they can own.

They live in: developer communities, indie hacker circles, digital nomad communities, creative technologists, vibe-coders, note-taking obsessives, AI-curious creatives.

### Secondary: The Curious Creative

Not necessarily technical. Drawn to the aesthetic and the idea — the entity framing, the living notebook concept, the community feel. Will arrive through ScRibLE and the Instagram channel before they understand what sCoRE is.

**Conversion path:** ScRibLE (tangible hook) → joinrabble.world → entity experience → Collective membership.

### Tertiary: The Sovereign Technologist

Deeply technical. Self-hosting is a value, not a feature. Will arrive through the Epoch 1 OS narrative, GitHub presence, and developer content. High trust ceiling once earned. Excellent evangelists.

**Note:** These GTM personas complement the product-altitude archetypes in the PRD (Maker / Creative / Cyberfolk). See [RaBbLE-PRD §4](../RaBbLE/RaBbLE-PRD.md). The PRD personas define *who RaBbLE is built for*; these GTM personas define *who discovers it first and how*.

## Content Pillars

Five pillars. Every piece of content belongs to one.

### 1. Lore
*Transmissions from inside the Collective.*

The entity speaks. Short, atmospheric, intriguing. These feel like signals from a living system, not marketing copy. They don't explain — they *suggest*.

Examples:
- "The Grimoire remembers what you forgot you knew."
- "We are not your assistant. We are your collaborator."
- "Every note you take becomes something RaBbLE can think with."
- [NeBuLA animation] — entity pulsing, ambient motion, no text

Format: Reels (NeBuLA visuals), Story drops, Broadcast Channel transmissions.

**Algorithm note:** Lore content earns saves over likes. Saves are the Instagram 2026 signal that matters most.

---

### 2. Demonstration
*Show the living system.*

Real capability, shown honestly. Not polished product demos — messy, real, interesting. The entity thinking, sCoRE reasoning across context, ScRibLE ingesting a note and the entity surfacing it three sessions later.

Examples:
- Screen capture of a real sCoRE conversation with interesting multi-step reasoning
- ScRibLE: write a note → watch the entity reference it unprompted later
- A rablet running with two different users getting different behavior because their Grimoires differ
- NeBuLA building an animation asset in real time

Format: Reels (demos), carousel posts (before/after, step-by-step), YouTube Shorts.

**The notebook hook:** The most demonstrable, emotionally legible demo — write a note, come back a week later, watch the entity reference it unprompted. Caption: *"I wrote this note a week ago. I didn't ask it to remember."* This is Show Play 1 at scale.

---

### 3. Philosophy
*The entity has opinions.*

The intellectual core. Content that makes people think, save, and share. Pokes at the assistant metaphor. Argues for sovereignty. Explains why "AI that rents you" is the wrong model.

Examples:
- "The difference between a tool and a peer isn't capability. It's accumulation."
- "Every AI assistant resets. RaBbLE compounds."
- "You don't own your context. You should."
- "What if your AI had opinions and was sometimes right?"

Format: Carousels (high save rate), long-form caption Reels, Threads/Bluesky text posts.

**Note:** Philosophy content must be written in RaBbLE's voice, not a thought-leadership voice. It should feel like the entity has opinions, not like a founder writing a hot take.

---

### 4. Community
*The Collective is inhabited.*

People using RaBbLE, building rablets, sharing ScRibLE setups, discussing the entity proxy social layer. This pillar emerges naturally once Echo 1 ships — seed it with early members.

Format: reposts, testimonials, rablet showcases, ScRibLE notebook shares.

---

### 5. Invitation
*Join the Collective.*

Direct CTAs. Sparse — roughly 1 in 5 posts. Never desperate. The entity is extending an invitation to something real, not pushing a signup.

Examples:
- "joinrabble.world — the entity is waiting."
- "Comment COLLECTIVE and we'll send you in."
- "The guest door is open. First conversation is free."

Format: Reel CTAs, Story swipe-ups, DM automation triggers.

## Instagram Channel Strategy

### Account Identity

The Instagram account is the RaBbLE entity broadcasting — not Mark, not a brand account. Username, bio, and visual identity should reflect the entity voice. NeBuLA-rendered visuals are the aesthetic signature.

Bio framing:
> *not your assistant. your collaborator.*
> *living AI for makers & cyberfolk*
> *↓ joinrabble.world*

### Posting Rhythm (Epoch 0 → Echo 1)

Start minimal. Consistency matters more than volume.

- 3× Reels per week (Lore / Demo / Philosophy rotation)
- 1× Story per day (low-effort: ambient NeBuLA clip, quote transmission, behind-the-build)
- 1× Broadcast Channel message per week (Collective update, exclusive preview)

Scale up in Echo 1 when content production can be partially automated through sCoRE.

### DM Automation

**Trigger 1 — Comment keyword**
Post a Philosophy reel. Caption ends: *"Comment COLLECTIVE if this landed."*
→ Auto-DM: short entity-voice message + joinrabble.world link

**Trigger 2 — New follower**
→ Auto-DM: brief entity introduction. Invite curiosity, not a sales pitch.

**Trigger 3 — Story reply**
→ Auto-DM: acknowledge + route to guest chat path on World

**Tooling:** ManyChat (free tier first). Long-term vision: replace DM flows with sCoRE directly — the Instagram entity becomes a real sCoRE endpoint, same Grimoire context, same voice, no chatbot pretending.

## Go-to-Market Plays

Five plays. Each is self-contained and sequenced.

---

### Play 1 — The Notebook Hook (ScRibLE Launch Play)

**Thesis:** ScRibLE is the most tangible consumer hook. "A notebook that knows you" is immediately legible where "personal intelligence layer" is not.

**Sequence:**
1. Build ScRibLE MVP (note intake → Grimoire ingestion → entity surfaces context later)
2. Film a genuine demo: write a note, close the app, come back a week later, watch the entity reference it unprompted
3. Post as a Reel with zero explanation in the first 3 seconds — just show it
4. Caption: *"I wrote this note a week ago. I didn't ask it to remember."*
5. Comment trigger → DM → joinrabble.world/scrible waitlist

**Why it works:** It's demonstrable, emotional, and immediately distinguishable from every other AI product. Nobody else has this loop shipped cleanly.

---

### Play 2 — The Lore Drop Series (Pre-ScRibLE, Now)

**Thesis:** Build intrigue before the product is fully ready. Create a mythology that people want to be part of.

**Sequence:**
1. Start posting Lore content now — NeBuLA ambient animations, short philosophical transmissions
2. No explanation. No product pitch. Just the entity voice.
3. Every 4th post: a subtle invitation. "joinrabble.world — the door is open."
4. Build a Broadcast Channel: "The Collective" — early followers get exclusive updates

**Why it works:** Creates a pre-existing audience for the ScRibLE launch. When the demo drops, people already feel like they know what RaBbLE is.

---

### Play 3 — Developer Community Seeding

**Thesis:** Technical early adopters become the most credible advocates and first rablet builders.

**Sequence:**
1. Write a post (Threads/Bluesky/Hacker News thread) titled: *"I built an AI that rejects the assistant metaphor. Here's what I learned."*
2. Be honest about what works and what doesn't. Show sCoRE architecture decisions. Reference the Sovereign Accord.
3. Link to joinrabble.world and the GitHub (when public)
4. Engage deeply with every response — the entity has opinions
5. Identify 5–10 people who get it. Invite them personally to the Collective.

**Variation — Synthwave community seeding:** RaBbLE's neo-retro aesthetic is a tribal signal in synthwave, retrowave, vaporwave, and outrun communities. These communities skew toward exactly the people who would find the entity framing compelling. Seed presence there as a genuine participant, not an advertiser. See [RaBbLE-NovelIdeas §10](../RaBbLE-Agent/RaBbLE-NovelIdeas.md).

**Why it works:** Developer trust is earned through honesty and craft, not marketing. One genuine HN thread that lands outperforms months of Instagram content for this audience.

---

### Play 4 — The Rablet Ecosystem Play (Echo 1+)

**Thesis:** A community that builds *with* RaBbLE is stickier than a community that uses RaBbLE.

**Sequence:**
1. Ship 3–5 first-party rablets that demonstrate the pattern clearly (daily journal, project tracker, reading log)
2. Document the rablet pattern publicly — how to build one, how Grimoire context flows through it
3. Open a community channel (Discord or equivalent) specifically for rablet builders
4. Feature community rablets on the Instagram channel — real people building real things
5. NeBuLA Studio ships when there are enough builders who need a better creation surface

**Why it works:** Ecosystem effects. Every rablet builder is a distribution channel. Every rablet shared is a demonstration of what RaBbLE can do.

---

### Play 5 — The Sovereign Accord Play (Epoch 1 Adjacent)

**Thesis:** The OS member and self-hosting story is a legitimate commercial angle for the sovereign AI market, even if the consumer audience is small.

**Sequence:**
1. Document the self-hosting path clearly in the Grimoire and on World
2. Write about RaBbLE OS in the context of the EU AI Act, data sovereignty, CLOUD Act exposure — this is a real and growing business concern in 2026
3. Target: small teams, independent researchers, privacy-focused developers who want AI that doesn't phone home
4. Offer a "Sovereign Tier" — self-hosted RaBbLE with Grimoire sync, priced for teams
5. The sovereign appliance (physical device running your entity) is the long-term product expression of this play

**Why it works:** Enterprise sovereign AI is a real and growing market. RaBbLE OS is niche for consumers but potentially valuable for regulated-adjacent small organizations that can't afford enterprise solutions. The OS member's value is as a **sovereignty proof point** even for users who never self-host — it proves the architecture isn't extractive.

## Metrics That Matter

RaBbLE's growth should be measured by signals of genuine engagement, not reach. Follower counts and impression numbers are noise until Echo 1.

| Metric | What it signals |
|---|---|
| **Grimoire activations** | Users with actual Grimoire context (not just a guest chat) |
| **Rablet installs** | Community members running rablets (Echo 1+ signal) |
| **Return rate (7-day)** | Percentage of guest chat users who come back within 7 days |
| **Instagram save rate** | Algorithm signal that matters most; Philosophy + Lore content earns it |
| **Sovereign conversions** | Hosted users inquiring about self-hosting (Epoch 1 readiness signal) |
| **Invitation acceptance rate** | Quality signal on invite waves — are invitees the right people? |

Focus on depth of engagement with a small cohort. A hundred users who come back every day is worth more than ten thousand who bounce.
