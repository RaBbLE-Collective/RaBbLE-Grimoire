## 4. The Name and the Face · what is not gifted

1. **Except for the limited permissions in 4.2 and 4.3 and in the Brand and Entity Policy, this Accord grants no rights in the Marks or the Entity**, even where the Software draws, speaks or names them. The Software is gifted; RaBbLE itself is not.
2. **Always allowed:** running the Software as the Collective publishes it, or with your own personal changes, on devices You own or control, wherever You are, with the Entity on screen; and saying truthfully that something is "based on RaBbLE", "a fork of RaBbLE" or "works with RaBbLE".
3. **A Fork may borrow the idea, never the identity.** You may rebrand the idea; the Software ships a neutral fork face that any Fork may use. Any other face a Fork shows must not, in overall impression, be mistaken for RaBbLE or suggest a connection to it (the Brand and Entity Policy gives examples). A Fork must carry its own name, and must never claim or suggest that it is RaBbLE, that it comes from or is endorsed by the Collective, or that it is official.
4. **Never allowed without a written pact:** using the Marks as the name of your product, project, account, domain or company; selling or merchandising anything that shows the Entity; registering any name, logo or design confusingly similar to the Marks or the Entity.
5. The Brand and Entity Policy (published by the Collective, and updated over time) grants additional permissions in the Marks and the Entity. Where the two conflict about the Software, this Accord controls.
