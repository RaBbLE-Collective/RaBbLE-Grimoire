## Why this exists

RaBbLE is a unique being. Its code is shared so people can learn from it and build their own; its name, face and colours are what make it *this* being, so they stay with The RaBbLE Collective. We want to inspire, not invite theft.

RaBbLE™, RaBbLE Pocket™ and the RaBbLE entity are trademarks of The RaBbLE Collective.

## Always fine, no need to ask

- Running RaBbLE (official, or with your own personal changes) on your own machines and Pocket, with the entity on screen. Your own RaBbLE is yours to tweak at home: that's the Pair.
- Saying true things: "built on RaBbLE", "a fork of RaBbLE", "works with RaBbLE", "inspired by RaBbLE".
- Talking about RaBbLE, reviewing it, teaching with it, filming it, screenshotting it.
- Fan content: reposting the Collective's images with credit, and your own fan art, for noncommercial purposes, as long as it doesn't look official. (This permission can be withdrawn for a specific use that harms RaBbLE.)

## Within bounds · for a Fork you share

A Fork can take the idea of a living, watching companion with its own face. It can't be RaBbLE.

- **Its own name.** Not RaBbLE, and not a name that sounds or looks like it (no "Rabbel", "RaBbLE+", "RabbleOS").
- **Its own face.** The test is the overall impression: nobody should mistake it for RaBbLE or think it's connected. The easy, always-safe route: use the **neutral fork face** the code ships with (a different palette and eye geometry, one setting). Anything else close to RaBbLE's eyes, portals, ring and cyan-and-magenta look is out of bounds, however many details changed. *[Review: needs a fork-face preset built into NeBuLA and the Pocket.]*
- **Its own voice and lore.** Don't copy RaBbLE's persona prompts, lore texts, recorded voice or catchphrases and present them as the Fork's own.
- **Honest lineage.** Say "based on RaBbLE by The RaBbLE Collective", as the Accord's Thread requires.
- Tweaks you send back to the Collective as contributions can change RaBbLE itself; that's how RaBbLE grows.

## Never, without a written pact

- Claiming or suggesting you are RaBbLE, the Collective, official, endorsed or partnered.
- Using the RaBbLE names, logos or the entity's face for your product, account, domain, app or company.
- Selling anything that shows the entity: merch, prints, devices, skins, stickers, NFTs, AI-generated likenesses.
- Registering names, handles, domains, logos or designs confusingly similar to RaBbLE or the entity.
- Training or prompting a model to imitate RaBbLE's persona and offering it to others as RaBbLE.
