# RaBbLE-Pocket: provenance

The Pocket firmware is original RaBbLE Collective work, released under the RaBbLE Sovereign Accord as described in the repository's `LICENSE` and `firmware/NOTICE`.

- **Third-party components and their licences** are listed in `firmware/NOTICE` and `third_party/README.md`. A small set of header interfaces carries its original copyright line and Apache-2.0 notice; the licence text is `firmware/LICENSE-APACHE`.
- **Provenance audit.** An independent provenance audit of the firmware was carried out (2026-11-02). The evidence is available on request.
- **History.** Earlier git history of RaBbLE-Pocket contains the modules as they were before they were rewritten, with their notices intact.

This page is a statement of provenance, not a legal opinion.
