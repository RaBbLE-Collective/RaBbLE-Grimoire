Poses come from poses.role.json, generated from the live tables. Each side takes the role its own portal bit gives it. Eyes hide only by receding into their own portal (blink is a short recede). The no-touch check is physical: portals are discs in their planes, eyes are upright pills, so an eye tip just over its portal does not touch it even where the drawing overlaps the ring. It is measured at rest, without blink or transitions. An eye is cut by its portal only once it starts to recede.
