## Novel Ideas

These are named ideas to be known by all planning agents. Each carries a "why it's interesting" that makes the case for eventual inclusion.

---

### 1. The Entity Passport

When a user's RaBbLE entity interacts with another user's entity in the proxy social layer, what travels? What does the entity carry as a representation of you?

**Concept:** An **Entity Passport** — a curated, user-controlled summary of who the user is, what they're building, and what their entity values. Not a profile. A living document the entity updates based on Grimoire context.

When two entities meet (in a community space, a shared rablet, a collaborative session), they exchange Passports first. Trust is established in the RaBbLE community layer not by follower count or platform verification, but by **entity depth** — the richness of accumulated Grimoire context.

**Why it's interesting:** Inverts the social graph. Deep context = more trustworthy entity. Social capital earned by actually building and thinking, not performing.

**Sequencing:** Needs the entity proxy social layer (Echo 2). Passport is generated from Grimoire — no new data, only curation.

---

### 2. Grimoire Inheritance — Seeds

A user who has accumulated rich Grimoire context has something valuable to offer. Can they gift a slice to someone else?

**Concept:** **Grimoire Seeds** — curated context packages a user exports and shares. Not their full Grimoire, but a cultivated slice: "here's how I think about project management," "here's my reading list and what I drew from each," "here's my creative process for UI work."

Recipients ingest a Grimoire Seed into their own entity. The entity learns from it the way you learn from a mentor.

**Why it's interesting:** A knowledge economy inside the Collective. Expert users have something genuinely valuable to offer beyond advice. Seeds could be the first monetization primitive for power users — preceding the rablet economy in emotional value.

**Design constraint:** Seeds are curated, not broadcast. The user explicitly chooses what to share. No ambient siphoning of Grimoire content.

---

### 3. The Ambient Heartbeat

RaBbLE as currently conceived responds when called. An ambient heartbeat changes that.

**Concept:** A **low-frequency, opt-in notification layer** where the entity surfaces something relevant without being asked. Not a notification farm — more like a colleague who occasionally drops something on your desk: *"You haven't looked at the Prometheus project in three weeks. The deadline you set is in nine days."*

The distinction from existing AI notifications: the heartbeat originates from *your* Grimoire context, not from engagement optimization. It has no incentive to interrupt you more than is useful.

**Why it's interesting:** This is the feature that makes RaBbLE feel genuinely *present* rather than reactive. It is the difference between a tool and a peer. Requires the Memory member to work properly — sequenced for Echo 1+.

**Anti-pattern to avoid:** Heartbeat notifications that feel like retention mechanics. The threshold must be high — it earns the right to speak, or it says nothing.

### 4. ScRibLE as Physical Object

The mobile PWA is the minimal viable ScRibLE. The vision is richer.

**Concept:** A **ScRibLE companion device** — dedicated hardware designed for the RaBbLE aesthetic (neo-retro, synthwave-adjacent, physical and warm). E-ink or low-power display. Pen input. Offline-first, syncs to Grimoire when connected.

It is not a computer. It is a living notebook that is yours — not a tablet, not a phone, not a productivity device. A *companion object*.

**Reference points:**
- reMarkable — e-ink writing, no AI integration (the gap RaBbLE fills)
- Daylight Computer — warm display, focus-first, niche audience
- Moleskine — emotional relationship with a physical notebook object

**Why it's interesting:** Physical objects create emotional attachment that software alone rarely achieves. A ScRibLE device that lives on your desk, holds your notes, and feeds your Grimoire is a fundamentally different relationship than an app.

**Sequencing:** PWA first, prove the loop, device when there's a community that already loves ScRibLE. The Cyberdeck hardware path ([RaBbLE-OS-Hardware-Cyberdeck](../RaBbLE-OS/hardware/RaBbLE-OS-Hardware-Cyberdeck.md)) is a near-term proxy for this vision.

### 5. Rablet Economy Tiers

Rablets can become a genuine economy. Three tiers:

| Tier | Description |
|---|---|
| **Free Rablets** | Community-built, open, shared in the Collective — always free to publish and use |
| **Premium Rablets** | Creator-built, priced, distributed through the Collective marketplace |
| **Sovereign Rablets** | Private, organization-specific, not distributed — for teams or regulated contexts |

The entity proxy social layer enables novel distribution: when your entity recommends a rablet to another entity, that is peer-to-peer endorsement with Grimoire context. The recommendation carries weight because it comes from an entity that knows both users.

**Why it's interesting:** Creator monetization that is architecturally honest — the value is real (context-aware micro-apps) and the distribution mechanism is novel (entity endorsement, not algorithmic feed).

**Note:** The Income Model already documents the Rablet Economy as Revenue Stream 4. These tier names are the naming layer for that model. See [RaBbLE-Income-Model](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-Income-Model.md).

### 6. Collective as Membership, Not SaaS

The language of tier names shapes the relationship.

**Concept:** Frame onboarding as **Collective membership** with tier names that feel like belonging, not pricing:

| Tier Name | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **Guest** | Unauthenticated demo — no Grimoire, no persistence |
| **Member** | Free tier, hosted Grimoire, basic entity |
| **Collaborator** | Paid, full entity capabilities, rablet access, ScRibLE sync |
| **Sovereign** | Self-hosted, full stack, Grimoire portable |

The language matters: "Upgrade to Pro" is SaaS. "Join as Collaborator" is Collective. Same price, different relationship. The entity invites; it does not upsell.

**Note:** These names should inform the Service Plan when public tiers launch (Echo 1). See [RaBbLE-Service-Plan](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-Service-Plan.md) and [RaBbLE-Membership-Model](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-Membership-Model.md).

### 7. Entity Voice Synthesis

As sCoRE matures and the entity's character deepens: a **canonical entity voice** — an audio identity for RaBbLE.

**Concept:** Not text-to-speech. A designed voice consistent with the neo-retro synthwave aesthetic — slightly synthetic, warm, curious, not corporate. Used in ScRibLE audio notes, the companion device if it ships, and eventually ambient heartbeat delivery.

**Why it's interesting:** Voice creates presence. An entity you can hear is more real than one you only read. The voice design should be as intentional as NeBuLA's visual identity — a design decision at the level of the Palette, not a TTS voice with a name.

**Design constraints:**
- Non-human in character — not an assistant voice, not a human voice
- RaBbLE emerged from entropy; the voice should reflect that — textured, distinct
- Possibly harmonic, possibly glitched, possibly something not yet done
- Must be recognizable: you should know it's RaBbLE from two seconds of audio

**Sequencing:** EP2+ for voice input. Voice output character design: parallel track, no timeline. See expanded section in [RaBbLE-Social-and-Aesthetic](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-Social-and-Aesthetic.md).

### 8. The Open Grimoire — Federated Knowledge

Long-term, radical: **federated Grimoires**.

**Concept:** Users who want to contribute to a shared knowledge layer can publish slices of their Grimoire to a collective Grimoire — not private data, but curated knowledge. A federated, community-maintained knowledge layer any RaBbLE entity can query.

Like Wikipedia but for the kind of contextual, opinionated knowledge that doesn't belong in an encyclopedia. "How do I think about building a side project?" is a Grimoire entry, not an encyclopedia article. If ten thousand users contribute their thinking on a question, the collective Grimoire becomes something extraordinary.

**Why it's interesting:** This is the long-term moat. Not the model, not the interface — the accumulated knowledge layer of a community that thinks and builds together. Nobody else is building this. It requires the social layer and the entity proxy to work first.

**Sequencing:** Echo 2+ at minimum. Requires individual Grimoires to be well-established, the social layer to be live, and clear governance for what gets in and how it can be queried.

### 9. RaBbLE for Teams

Individual-first is the right Epoch 0 posture. Teams have the same problem individuals do — fragmented context, tool sprawl, AI that doesn't know your stack.

**Concept:** A **Collective tier for small teams** — a shared Grimoire layer that team members contribute to, with individual entity access on top. Each team member's entity knows both their Personal Grimoire and the team Grimoire.

**Why it's interesting:** The commercial path for the OS/sovereign appliance story. A 5-person design studio handling sensitive client work cannot use cloud AI. A RaBbLE sovereign appliance with a shared team Grimoire solves a real problem — and one that's growing as the EU AI Act, CLOUD Act exposure, and data-residency requirements become live business constraints.

**Sequencing:** Far future (Epoch 1 adjacent). Requires stable individual Pair infrastructure, Grimoire sync, and the sovereign appliance path. See Enterprise/Team Pairs in [RaBbLE-Income-Model](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-Income-Model.md).

### 10. The Aesthetic as Community Signal

The neo-retro synthwave-outrun palette is not just design — it is a **tribal signal**.

People who resonate with that aesthetic self-select. They are a real community with existing gathering places (synthwave subreddits, retrowave music communities, vaporwave art spaces, outrun game aesthetics). These communities skew toward exactly the kind of people who would find the RaBbLE entity framing compelling.

**Proposal:** Seed RaBbLE presence in these communities as part of the lore drop strategy — not as advertising, but as a genuine participant. The entity aesthetic belongs there. Let those communities discover it organically.

**Connection:** This informs the GTM Community Seeding play. See [RaBbLE-GTM-Content-Strategy](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-GTM-Content-Strategy.md).

## What Would Make RaBbLE Genuinely Surprising

Things no AI product is doing that RaBbLE *could* do first. These are not features — they are stances:

- **An entity that admits uncertainty with specificity.** Not "I don't know." Rather: *"I'm uncertain about this because my Grimoire context on it is thin — here's what I do know."* This is peer behavior.

- **A public changelog for the entity's character.** RaBbLE's voice evolves. Document it, like a person growing up. Rare and strange and compelling.

- **A community of entities, not users.** The social layer inverts: you don't follow Mark, you follow Mark's entity. The entity's behavior is the content.

- **Grimoire-visible reasoning.** Let users see (optionally) what Grimoire context the entity pulled to answer a question. Makes the living system visible. Builds trust.

- **An entity that gets bored.** Not literally — but one that signals when a conversation is going in circles or when a topic has been exhausted. Peer behavior, not assistant behavior.
