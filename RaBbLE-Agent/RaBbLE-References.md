## Agentic Architecture

**BuilderIO / agent-native**
`https://github.com/BuilderIO/agent-native`

Demonstrates an agentic-first architecture for building software. Relevant to the RaBbLE Collective's goal of making RaBbLE an ambient agent that acts on intent rather than waiting for explicit commands. Study for patterns around agentic delegation, tool orchestration, and agent-to-agent coordination.

---

## Component Portability

**Mitosis — BuilderIO**
`https://mitosis.builder.io/`

Write components once, compile to React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, SolidJS, and more. Directionally relevant to RaBbLE-NeBuLA's long-term Qt/QML portability goal — the same "write-once, target-many" thinking applies to rendering backends (Canvas2D, Three.js, WebGPU, C++/OpenGL, QML). Not a direct dependency; a pattern reference.
