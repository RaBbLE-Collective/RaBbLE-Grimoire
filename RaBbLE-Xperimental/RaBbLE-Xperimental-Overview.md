## Role

`RaBbLE-Xperimental` is the **active creative sandbox** — the space for things that have not yet emerged. Custom rablets in development, prototype members, experiments not ready for their own repo. High entropy by design.

This is not the genesis archive. That is [RaBbLE-Chrysalis](../RaBbLE-Chrysalis/RaBbLE-Chrysalis-Overview.md).

---

## What Xperimental Holds

| Content | Description |
|---|---|
| **Rablets in development** | Custom apps/tools being authored by a Pair before publishing |
| **Prototype members** | Experiments that may graduate to a full Collective member repo |
| **Sandboxed experiments** | "What if we tried X?" — no commitment required |
| **Dev parts not yet landed** | Components or scripts belonging to a member but not ready to merge |

---

## Operating Rules

- **High entropy is correct** — this is the designed purpose
- **Nothing is permanent** — content graduates or gets released
- **Graduation paths:**
  - Rablet ready to publish → Published Rablets layer in Personal Cosmos
  - Project ready to become a member → `bash spells/init-project.sh --slug RaBbLE-[Name]`
- **Branch structure:** `main` + `feature/*` per experiment
- **No archive branches here** — completed/deprecated work goes to Chrysalis's Reliquary
- **Receives Grimoire doc propagation:** no — too high-entropy for canonical docs

---

## Relationship to Personal Cosmos

Xperimental is the Collective-level sandbox. Each Pair also has a personal Xperimental space within their Personal Cosmos (see [Personal Cosmos](../RaBbLE-Collective/RaBbLE-Personal-Cosmos.md)). The two are distinct:

| Xperimental (Collective) | Xperimental (Personal Cosmos) |
|---|---|
| Shared dev sandbox for the Collective | Private sandbox per Pair |
| Rablets intended for the wider Collective | Personal experiments and drafts |
| Can graduate to a full member repo | Graduates to the Pair's Published Rablets |
